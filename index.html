About these figures, disclosure and right of reply

Figures come from official statistics (NBS, CBN, DMO, NUPRC, NERC), international institutions (IMF, World Bank) and independent trackers and polls, many as reported in the Nigerian press. Methods changed during the period: GDP was rebased in 2025 and the consumer price index in 2025, so figures before and after are not strictly comparable. Some household prices are author-supplied from market observation and word of mouth and are labelled that way.

This independent research project is an initiative of Bolaji Moyo, created to improve public understanding of Nigeria's economic and social conditions under President Bola Ahmed Tinubu. It is designed to outlast the current administration, so that Nigerians can compare the performance of successive presidents on the same measures.

Bolaji Moyo sets the research direction, poses the critical questions and exercises editorial oversight. Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, supports data gathering, analysis, comparison, explanation and visual presentation.

The project is not commissioned, funded, affiliated with or endorsed by any political party, candidate or government institution, and it does not endorse any candidate, party or voting choice. Its purpose is to present verifiable evidence, distinguish government claims from measurable outcomes, and help readers reach their own conclusions.

Every statistic, interpretation and visualisation is tied to an identifiable source. Consequential AI-generated analysis is checked by the author against its sources, and any unresolved limitations are disclosed.

Right of reply: any member of the public may submit a correction with evidence; material corrections appear in the next edition. Send corrections and responses to why9ija@gmail.com. Data are refreshed on the author's request. Full report and data: bolajimoyo.com.
